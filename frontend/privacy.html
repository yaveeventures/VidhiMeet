Trust & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

At VidhiMeet, we believe privacy is a fundamental promise. As a marketplace provider connecting clients with verified legal professionals, we handle highly sensitive legal matters. This policy details how we handle, secure, and protect your information, with special compliance guidelines for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information We Collect

To deliver a highly functional marketplace, we collect two types of information:

  • Basic Profile Data: Names, email addresses, and contact numbers. Lawyer profiles also store verified credential records, practice details, and verified government IDs.
  • Consultation Metadata & Documents: Intake form answers, dates/times of consultations, chat messages, and legal case documents.

2. End-to-End Encryption & Security

Unlike standard legal directories, VidhiMeet provides integrated secure tooling:

  • Secure Video Rooms: All consultation calls are run through our custom end-to-end encrypted Daily video service, bypassing public logs.
  • Secure Vault: All uploaded case documents and records are encrypted at rest using industry-standard keys. Access is restricted exclusively to you and your assigned lawyer.
  • Chat Security: Instant text messaging within the app uses localized secure key derivation, meaning conversations are encrypted.

3. Consent and Data Processing (DPDP Act 2023)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you retain absolute authority over your personal information:

  • We process your data strictly on the grounds of explicit consent for executing your scheduled lawyer consultations.
  • You have the right to withdraw consent at any time, request structural corrections, or request complete erasure of your platform records.

4. Data Retention

We retain transaction details and consultation histories to settle payouts, tax logs, and dispute records. Personal case documentation and secure chat records can be purged at any time at the request of the client.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may modify this policy periodically to align with updated safety policies. Changes will be updated on this page with a revised date stamp.

6. Contact Us

For support requests or details on your data rights, contact us at privacy@vidhimeet.in.
