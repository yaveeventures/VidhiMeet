3. Consultations & Payments

  • Booking Fees: Clients pay the designated consultation fee at the time of booking. The funds are held securely in our marketplace escrow system.

4. Dispute Handling, Metadata Waiver & 3-Step Resolution Matrix

If a consultation fails due to technical connection issues, failure of attendance, or standard dispute cases, the client or lawyer may click Dispute inside their consultation list within 24 hours before funds are released. Upon initiation of a dispute:

  • Escrow Hold: Funds remain frozen in escrow and payout is automatically paused.
  • Metadata Waiver & Automated Session Telemetry: Clients and lawyers agree that in the event of a dispute, the platform's automated room connection logs (timestamps and participant durations from Daily.co telemetry) will serve as the sole and definitive evidence to determine refund eligibility.
  • Automated Resolution Rules:
    • Lawyer No-Show: If connection logs show the lawyer duration was 0 minutes, a full refund is automatically issued to the client, and a strike/warning is logged against the lawyer's profile.
    • Connection Drop / Brief Call (<5 mins or <50% duration): If network drops or brief attendance occur, funds are held and full refund or reschedule is granted.
    • Full Attendance (≥50% duration): If both parties were present for 50% or more of the slot time, the platform's Intermediary Shield applies. Claims regarding subjective quality of legal advice are governed by Section 79 rules—the platform does not guarantee legal outcomes, and escrow is released to the advocate.
