About VidhiMeet

A digital legal technology initiative by YAVEE VENTURES PRIVATE LIMITED

VidhiMeet is an Indian legal technology platform built with a singular mission: to make verified legal counsel accessible, transparent, and dignified for every citizen and business across India. Whether you are navigating complex property registrations, drafting enterprise agreements, resolving family disputes, or incorporating a startup, VidhiMeet connects you with verified legal practitioners from the comfort and privacy of your home or office.

Who We Are

VidhiMeet is conceived, owned, and operated by YAVEE VENTURES PRIVATE LIMITED, an incorporated company registered under the Companies Act, 2013 in Karnataka, India. We operate strictly as an Electronic Marketplace Intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable Intermediary Guidelines. VidhiMeet is not a law firm and does not practice law; we engineer state-of-the-art software infrastructure that empowers certified advocates and clients to collaborate securely.
